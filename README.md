## 🚀 Getting Started
Welcome to the heart-disease-prediction-ann project! This software helps you predict the likelihood of heart disease using advanced methods. You can analyze important health data, such as age, cholesterol levels, and blood pressure.

## 📋 Prerequisites
Before you begin, make sure your computer has:
- Windows, macOS, or Linux operating system.
- At least 4 GB of RAM.
- 500 MB of free disk space.
- An internet connection for downloading the software.

## 🔍 Features
The heart-disease-prediction-ann application offers:
- Detailed exploratory data analysis (EDA) on heart disease datasets.
- Models including Artificial Neural Networks (ANN), Random Forest, Decision Trees, and Support Vector Machines (SVM) for predictions.
- Visualization tools to help you understand the data.
- Performance metrics to evaluate model accuracy, such as F1-score, precision, and recall.

After installing, you will find an icon for the application on your desktop. 

1. Open the application by double-clicking the icon.
2. Upload your heart disease data file in CSV format.
3. Select the model you wish to use: ANN, Random Forest, Decision Tree, or SVM.
4. Click the “Predict” button.
5. View predictions along with important visualizations and metrics.
